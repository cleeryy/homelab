OPNsense is the edge: double-NATted behind the ISP box (`192.168.1.250/24` on `vtnet0`), trunking seven VLANs plus LAN out of `vtnet1` into `vmbr1`. WireGuard road-warrior (`10.1.90.0/24`), AirVPN client with kill-switch, NetBird mesh, CrowdSec bouncer, IDS on WAN/PROD/MGMT. DNS: Unbound (`:53`) forwarding `internal` to dnsmasq (`:53053`) and `internal.cleeryy.cloud` to Technitium (`10.1.50.53`).

## Placement

- VM `501` `opnsense` on `pve3` — master, running, 16 GB RAM, `net0` vmbr0 + `net1` vmbr1 trunk.
- VM `502` `opnsense-slave` on `pve1` — full clone (28/09/2026), **stopped**, `onboot 0`, new MACs. CARP/pfsync to be configured together.

## Status

- Master running. No `hasync`, no CARP VIPs yet — single node. Slave must never boot unconfigured (IP conflict with master).

## Dependencies

- ISP box (uplink), `nas` (VM disks), Cloudflare (DNS-01, tunnels).

## Failure impact

- Master down = no inter-VLAN routing, no internet for VLANs, no SSO-gated ingress. The standby exists precisely for this; finish CARP.

## Known gaps

- CARP VIPs per VLAN + pfsync + XMLRPC sync are designed, not applied. Inter-VLAN policy is still allow-all (`[DEV]`/`[TEMP]`).
