title: TrueNAS
description: Storage VM deliberately staying on the LAN on `pve3`.
---

TrueNAS is the exception that proves the migration: while everything else moved to VLAN 20/50, the storage VM stays on `192.168.1.74` (`vmbr0`) with passed-through disks, because both Proxmox NFS storages and in-guest `/mnt/medias` mounts point at LAN addresses. Moving it buys nothing and risks every mount in the fleet.

## Placement

- VM `127` on `pve3`, `192.168.1.74` (vmbr0). QEMU guest agent running.

## Status

- Running. Exports consumed as PVE `truenas` storage and guest NFS mounts. Reachable from VLAN 20 via OPNsense (same path as the physical NAS).

## Dependencies

- Passed-through physical disks (see nodes gaps: slot mapping unknown).

## Failure impact

- NFS-backed guests lose media/storage mounts until restore. VM virtual disks on `nas` are unaffected.

## Known gaps

- Which physical disk sits in which slot, and the TrueNAS pool/dataset layout, are not recorded here.
