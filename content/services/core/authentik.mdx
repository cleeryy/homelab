## Ingress

- `authentik.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `https://authentik:9443` (`tls_insecure_skip_verify`, self-signed backend).
- Outpost endpoint `authentik:9000` serves Caddy `forward_auth` (`/outpost.goauthentik.io/auth/caddy`), currently wired on `code.cleeryy.cloud` with `X-Authentik-*` headers forwarded.

## Role

Authentik is the identity provider for humans (SSO logins) and for services (Caddy `forward_auth` gates, Warpgate bastion authentication). It is in the critical backup job (02:00, keep-last 7) — losing it locks out every gated route.

## Dependencies

- Reachable from Caddy (`2010`) and from gated upstreams over the production VLAN.
- Database backend: not exported into this documentation (likely the shared PostgreSQL `112`; pending validation).

## Failure impact

- Authentik down → SSO logins fail and every `forward_auth` route returns 401/403. Ungated public routes are unaffected.
- A `pve3` outage takes Authentik with it.
