Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

Gamevault is the elephant in the cluster: a single guest holding over a terabyte of game data, bigger than everything else combined. It gets its own page for exactly one reason — to record, loudly, that it is excluded from backups on purpose. Backing up 1.2 TB of re-downloadable binaries every night would drown the backup server for zero benefit, so the recovery plan is reinstallation, not restoration. If disk pressure ever forces a conversation about what gets deleted first, start here: nothing on this host is precious.

## Placement

Gamevault runs as LXC `258` (`gamevault`) on `pve3` on the production VLAN, carrying the `10.1.20.58` tag with 4 GB of RAM and roughly 1.2 TB of about 1.8 TB disk in use — the largest single guest on the cluster.

## Ingress

The service is reached as `gamevault.cleeryy.cloud` (public) plus its downloader route, served through Caddy like the other apps.

## Dependencies

Backend storage for the game library lives on the LXC disk, though the exact mount and volume layout is not captured here. There is no database or shared service in the path — just a very large pile of files on local disk.

## Backup posture

Gamevault is **deliberately excluded** from all PBS jobs: some 1.2 TB of replaceable game data. Re-download, don't restore. This exclusion is by design, not an oversight, and it is the single most important fact on this page.

## Failure impact

A `pve3` outage takes Gamevault down with the production VLAN. Recovery means re-deployment plus re-download, potentially slow on large libraries — the accepted price of not backing up a terabyte of binaries.

## Known gaps

Volume layout, library paths, and a re-seed time estimate remain undocumented — the three facts a rebuild would need, none of them urgent since nothing here is precious. The day disk pressure arrives, this page becomes important; until then it is deliberately thin.
