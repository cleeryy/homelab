Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

## Placement

- LXC `258` `gamevault` on `pve3` (production VLAN, `10.1.20.58` tag; 4 GB RAM, ~1.2 TB of ~1.8 TB disk in use — the largest single guest on the cluster).

## Ingress

`gamevault.cleeryy.cloud` (public) plus downloader route; served through Caddy like other apps.

## Dependencies

- Backend storage for the game library lives on the LXC disk. Exact mount/volume layout not captured here.

## Backup posture

**Deliberately excluded** from all PBS jobs: ~1.2 TB of replaceable game data. Re-download, don't restore. This exclusion is by design, not an oversight.

## Failure impact

- A `pve3` outage takes Gamevault down. Recovery is re-deployment + re-download, potentially slow on large libraries.
