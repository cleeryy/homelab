TaoXD is a stopped 4 GB / 64 GB VM on the production VLAN tag with no documentation, no DNS record, and no owner on file. It was running at the start of the September migration and stopped at some point during it; whether that was cleanup or accident is itself undocumented. A stopped unknown is the cheapest kind of mystery to keep and the most annoying kind to resolve — it costs nothing while off, but nobody can say whether booting or deleting it is the right move.

## Placement

The guest is VM `2242` on `pve3`, with `net0` on `vmbr1` tag 20. It is **stopped**.

## Status

The VM is stopped. The QEMU guest agent flag is set and takes effect on next boot, whenever that boot happens.

## Dependencies

Its dependencies are unknown — nothing was recorded about what it consumed or what consumed it while it ran.

## Failure impact

No impact has been observed while it sits stopped. Whatever it once did, nothing currently running appears to miss it.

## Known gaps

Everything: what it ran, who stopped it, whether to boot or delete it. This page is a placeholder until someone claims it — boot it to identify it, or delete it to end the suspense.
