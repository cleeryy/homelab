Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

Preproduction is where experiments live before they earn the right to touch real data. The deal is simple: anything here may break, may be wiped, and must never be depended on by a production service. The preproduction VLAN exists precisely to make that promise enforceable at the network layer, even though today only one guest actually lives on it. If you are about to test something risky, this is where it goes — not on the production VLAN next to Immich and Nextcloud.

## Purpose

Project-specific hosts that are not part of the production data path. `projet-paul` is the only guest currently living on the preproduction VLAN, which otherwise sits idle.

## Placement

- All pre-production workloads live on `pve3`, separated from production stacks by VLAN (for `257`) or by convention.

## Operational notes

- These are lab workloads. They are not part of the production data path and should not be relied on by user-facing services.
- Backup posture: covered by the general PBS job; per-guest restore priority is best-effort.
