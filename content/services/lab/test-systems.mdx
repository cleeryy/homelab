Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

Not everything on the cluster is production, and this page is the proof. Development environments, agent sandboxes, and parked machines live here under one rule: they can change or vanish without notice, and nobody's evening is ruined when they do. Stopped guests stay listed anyway, because a powered-off VM still occupies disk, an ID, and someone's vague intention — calling them out is what turns good intentions into decommissioning decisions.

## Placement

All lab and test workloads live on `pve3`, kept distinct from the production stacks by host convention and, where it matters, by VLAN.

## Operational notes

Stopped systems are documented because their existence is part of the architecture: they are decision items, not active workloads, as tracked in the [migration plan](/architecture/migration-plan). These systems are **not** monitored as production, and their state can change without notice — treat every address here as advisory.
