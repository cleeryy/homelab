Guacamole is the browser-based remote desktop gateway (RDP/VNC/SSH through HTML5). MySQL (`:3306` loopback) holds its connection definitions.

## Placement

- LXC `106` `apache-guacamole` on `pve3`, `10.1.20.48:8080` (VLAN 20).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `guacamole.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `apache-guacamole:8080`.

## Dependencies

- Local MySQL only. Target machines are reached over the tailnet/VLANs.

## Failure impact

- No browser remote access until restore. Direct SSH via Warpgate still works.

## Known gaps

- SSO (Authentik) is not in front of Guacamole yet — see the SSO coverage item in the [migration plan](/architecture/migration-plan).
