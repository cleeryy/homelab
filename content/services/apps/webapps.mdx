`ntfy` (Proxmox name `ubuntu-small-webapps`) is the small-webapps Docker host. Despite the Beszel hostname, it is not the notification service — it is four small stacks behind one IP.

## Placement

- VM `102` on `pve1`, `10.1.20.42` (VLAN 20, netplan static, QEMU guest agent running).

## Status

- Running. Ports: `:8282` (tools), `:4001` (overleaf), `:8888` (vw), `:4096` (backup-ntfy) — all via Caddy.

## Dependencies

- None external. Images come from public registries.

## Failure impact

- Four small apps down at once until restore. Guest-level PBS backup applies.

- Which ISO/upstream each of the four stacks tracks is not recorded here.
