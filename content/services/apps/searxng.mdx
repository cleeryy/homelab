Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

A search box that answers to nobody. SearXNG forwards your queries to a spread of upstream engines and mixes the results, so no single provider gets the full picture of what you look for. There is no account, no history worth stealing, and nothing to back up — the whole service is disposable by design. If it ever misbehaves, the fix is measured in minutes: check the engine list for a blocked upstream, or just redeploy it.

## Placement

The service runs as LXC `132` (`searxng`) on `pve3` on the production VLAN, carrying the `10.1.20.46` tag with 2 GB of RAM.

## Ingress

Traffic arrives as `search.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `searxng:8888`, with no SSO gate — a conscious non-priority, since there is no user data here worth gating.

## Dependencies

The only dependencies are the upstream search engines declared in the SearXNG settings. Rate-limiting or an engine block degrades result quality without taking the service down, which is exactly the failure mode metasearch is built to absorb.

## Failure impact

A `pve3` outage takes SearXNG down with the production VLAN, but there is nothing to lose: the service is stateless and rebuilds from its engine list.

## Known gaps

Only the engine list, plus any private-engine credentials, is undocumented. This is about as finished as a service page gets.
