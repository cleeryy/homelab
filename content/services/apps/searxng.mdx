## Placement

- LXC `132` `searxng` on `pve3` (production VLAN, `10.1.20.46` tag; 2 GB RAM).

## Ingress

`search.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `searxng:8888`. No SSO gate currently.

## Dependencies

- Upstream search engines (declared in the SearXNG settings); rate-limiting or engine blocks degrade results without taking the service down.

## Failure impact

- A `pve3` outage takes SearXNG down; stateless — nothing to lose.
