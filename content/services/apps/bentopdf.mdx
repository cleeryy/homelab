title: BentoPDF
description: PDF utility service on `pve1`.

BentoPDF is the PDF toolbox of the homelab: merge, split, compress, and convert through a plain web UI, no accounts and nothing to learn. It follows the same philosophy as the other small utilities — one container, one port, tucked behind Caddy — so it is trivial to run and trivial to replace. There is no state worth protecting here beyond the running container itself.

## Placement

The service runs as LXC `104` (`bentopdf`) on `pve1`, listening at `10.1.20.37:8080` on VLAN 20.

## Status

The container is running. Ingress is `pdf.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `10.1.20.37:8080`.

## Dependencies

BentoPDF depends on nothing: no database, no shared storage, no upstream service.

## Failure impact

If the LXC fails, the PDF tools are simply unreachable until the guest is restored. No downstream service depends on it, so the blast radius ends at the toolbox itself.

## Known gaps

Upload size limits and temp-file cleanup behavior are not recorded here — the two practical details that decide whether large PDFs succeed or fill the disk.
