BentoPDF is the PDF toolbox: merge, split, compress and convert through a web UI. Same philosophy as the other utilities — one container, one port, behind Caddy.

## Placement

- LXC `104` `bentopdf` on `pve1`, `10.1.20.37:8080` (VLAN 20).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `pdf.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `10.1.20.37:8080`.

## Dependencies

- None.

## Failure impact

- PDF tools unreachable until restore. No downstream services.

## Known gaps

- Upload size limits and temp-file cleanup are not recorded here.
