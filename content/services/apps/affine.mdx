AFFiNE is the collaborative docs/whiteboard workspace. It runs with its own Postgres (`:5432` loopback) and Redis (`:6379` loopback) inside the container — self-contained by design.

## Placement

- LXC `126` `affine` on `pve3`, `10.1.20.64:80` (VLAN 20).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `affine.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `affine:3010` (short-name DNS via Technitium).

## Dependencies

- Local Postgres + Redis only. No external database.

## Failure impact

- Workspace unreachable until restore. Application data lives in the container (covered by the general PBS job).

## Known gaps

- Application-level export/backup story beyond guest-level PBS is unconfirmed.
