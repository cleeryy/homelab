title: AFFiNE
description: Collaborative workspace on `pve3`.

AFFiNE is the collaborative workspace of the homelab: shared docs, whiteboards, and notes in a single web UI. It runs self-contained by design, with its own Postgres on loopback `:5432` and Redis on loopback `:6379` inside the same container, so there is no external database to wire or to lose. That simplicity is the whole point — one guest to back up, one guest to restore — at the cost that everything the workspace knows lives inside that container until someone proves otherwise.

## Placement

The service lives as LXC `126` (`affine`) on `pve3`, addressed at `10.1.20.64:80` on VLAN 20.

## Status

The container is running. Public traffic arrives as `affine.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `affine:3010`, resolved via short-name DNS through Technitium.

## Dependencies

There are no external dependencies to track: Postgres and Redis both run locally inside the container, so AFFiNE does not depend on the shared PostgreSQL LXC or on any other service.

## Failure impact

If the container fails, the workspace is simply unreachable until the guest is restored from backup. Application data lives inside the container itself and is covered by the general PBS job, so guest-level restore is the recovery path.

## Known gaps

The one open question is the application-level export and backup story beyond guest-level PBS: whether workspace content can be exported or must be recovered whole-container is unconfirmed.
