Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

With dozens of hostnames in the air, nobody remembers them all — Dynacat is the start page that remembers for you. One dashboard with a tile per service turns the whole homelab into something guests and family can actually use without a guided tour. It holds no data and fronts nothing sensitive, so it sits deliberately low in every priority list on this site: nice to have, trivial to rebuild, first thing to check when someone says "what do we run here, actually".

## Placement

Dynacat runs as LXC `151` (`dynacat`) on `pve3` on the production VLAN, carrying the `10.1.20.51` tag with 2 GB of RAM.

## Ingress

Public traffic arrives as `dashboard.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `dynacat:8080`, currently with no SSO gate — a deliberate non-priority for a page of links.

## Role

Dynacat is a start-page dashboard linking the homelab services. It is stateless by nature; its configuration — the tile list itself — is the only asset worth keeping.

## Failure impact

If Dynacat goes down, operators lose the start page and guests lose the friendly map, but every service remains directly reachable at its own address. The outage is an inconvenience, never an emergency.

## Known gaps

Where the dashboard definition lives, and therefore how to restore it, is undocumented — one sentence answering that would finish this page. SSO remains a conscious non-decision rather than an oversight.
