`code` is the workshop where this very wiki is written: the `homelab` Fumadocs repository lives at `/root/homelab` inside this LXC and gets edited here. Alongside the editor it hosts side services on `:3000` (arneph), `:4096` (opencodeeee), and `:8680`, which makes it both a dev box and a small app host. Losing it would be annoying rather than catastrophic — editing stops, but the published site keeps serving because Dokploy builds and serves it independently.

## Placement

The environment is LXC `119` (`code`) on `pve3`, addressed at `10.1.20.50` on VLAN 20.

## Status

The LXC is running. Ingress is `code.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `code:8680` behind Authentik `forward_auth`, with `arneph` reaching `10.1.20.50:3000` and `opencodeeee` reaching `10.1.20.50:4096`.

## Dependencies

The only real dependency is the git remote for the wiki repository: work done inside this LXC persists only once it is pushed, so an unpushed edit session is the one thing a host loss would actually destroy.

## Failure impact

A failure here stops doc edits and the side services until the guest is restored. The published wiki is unaffected, since it is built and served by Dokploy rather than from this container.

## Known gaps

What arneph and opencodeeee actually do is not recorded here — their purpose, owner, and whether they deserve ingress of their own are all open.
