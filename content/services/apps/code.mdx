`code` is the dev environment where the `homelab` Fumadocs repository lives (`/root/homelab`) and gets edited. It also runs side services on `:3000` (arneph), `:4096` (opencodeeee) and `:8680`.

## Placement

- LXC `119` `code` on `pve3`, `10.1.20.50` (VLAN 20).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `code.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `code:8680` behind Authentik `forward_auth`; `arneph` → `10.1.20.50:3000`, `opencodeeee` → `10.1.20.50:4096`.

## Dependencies

- Git remote for the wiki repo (push to persist work done here).

## Failure impact

- No doc edits or side services until restore. The published wiki (built by Dokploy) keeps serving.

## Known gaps

- What arneph and opencodeeee are for is not recorded here.
