Synapse is the Matrix homeserver; Element (`:5173`) is the web client served next to it. Federation port `8448` applies if federation is ever enabled — until then this is a private chat island behind Caddy.

## Placement

- LXC `107` `elementsynapse` on `pve1`, `10.1.20.38:8008` (VLAN 20).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `matrix.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `10.1.20.38:8008`.

## Dependencies

- Database backend (SQLite or Postgres) is inside the container; exact wiring unconfirmed.

## Failure impact

- Chat history unreachable until restore. No other service depends on it.

## Known gaps

- Confirm the database backend and whether federation (`:8448`) is intended.
