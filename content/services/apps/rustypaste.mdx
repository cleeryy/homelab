---
title: RustyPaste
description: Minimal file/pastebin utility on `pve1`.
---

RustyPaste does one thing — accept a file or a paste through a tiny web UI and hand back a link. No accounts, no database, nothing to back up beyond the container itself. It lives on an Alpine LXC because it needs almost nothing to run, and that frugality is its resilience: there is barely anything here that can break.

## Placement

The service runs as LXC `101` (`alpine-rustypaste`) on `pve1`, listening at `10.1.20.35:8000` on VLAN 20 via gateway `10.1.20.1`.

## Status

The container is running. Ingress is `paste.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `10.1.20.35:8000`.

## Dependencies

RustyPaste depends on nothing. It is stateless apart from the pasted files sitting on local disk, so its only consumption is a little disk space.

## Failure impact

If the LXC fails, pastes are unreachable until the guest is restored. Nothing else depends on it, so the outage begins and ends at the pastebin.

## Known gaps

Retention and expiry policy plus the maximum upload size are not recorded here — the two settings that decide whether this stays a handy scratch space or quietly fills its disk.
