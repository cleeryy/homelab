---
title: RustyPaste
description: Minimal file/pastebin utility on `pve1`.
---

RustyPaste does one thing — accept a file or a paste through a tiny web UI and hand back a link. No accounts, no database, nothing to back up beyond the container itself. It lives on an Alpine LXC because it needs almost nothing to run.

## Placement

- LXC `101` `alpine-rustypaste` on `pve1`, `10.1.20.35:8000` (VLAN 20, gw `10.1.20.1`).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `paste.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `10.1.20.35:8000`.

## Dependencies

- None. Stateless apart from pasted files on local disk.

## Failure impact

- Pastes unreachable until the LXC is restored. Nothing else depends on it.

## Known gaps

- Retention/expiry policy and max upload size are not recorded here.
