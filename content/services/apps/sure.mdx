Sure is the finance app, backed by local Postgres (`:5432` loopback) and Redis (`:6379` loopback) inside its container.

## Placement

- LXC `128` `sure` on `pve3`, `10.1.20.54:3000` (VLAN 20).

## Status

- Running. Ingress: `sure.cleeryy.cloud` → Caddy → `sure:3000` (short-name DNS).

## Dependencies

- Local Postgres + Redis only.

## Failure impact

- Finance data unreachable until restore. Guest-level PBS backup applies.

## Known gaps

- Export/backup procedure for the financial data is not recorded here.
