Sure is the finance app: the ledger nobody wants to rebuild from memory. Like AFFiNE it carries its own Postgres on loopback `:5432` and Redis on loopback `:6379` inside its container, self-contained rather than wired into shared services. That makes the host easy to reason about and nerve-wracking to lose, since financial history is exactly the kind of data whose absence is discovered at the worst possible moment.

## Placement

The app runs as LXC `128` (`sure`) on `pve3`, listening at `10.1.20.54:3000` on VLAN 20.

## Status

The container is running. Ingress is `sure.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy toward `sure:3000`, resolved via short-name DNS.

## Dependencies

Sure depends only on its local Postgres and Redis. There is no external database or shared service in the path.

## Failure impact

If the LXC fails, finance data is unreachable until the guest is restored. Guest-level PBS backup applies, so recovery is whole-container restore — adequate, but untested at the application level.

## Known gaps

The export and backup procedure for the financial data is not recorded here. For a finance app, proving that restore path is worth more than any amount of uptime.
