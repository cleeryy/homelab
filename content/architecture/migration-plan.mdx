Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

This page captures **transitions that are already visible** in the live setup, not aspirational moves. It is short by design — and it is also the scoreboard. The resolved section at the top is deliberately kept, not deleted: it proves the homelab finishes what it starts, and it keeps future readers from re-litigating settled decisions. Something in flight belongs here from the day it is observed, with an owner and a next action, until the day it moves up into resolved.

## Resolved since June 2026

- **Cluster `pve2`.** Fully removed — no member, no `pve2-storage` entry. Closed.
- **Network `vmbr2`.** The bridge no longer exists on `pve3`. Closed.
- **Ingress NPM.** Nginx Proxy Manager (VM `100`) decommissioned; **Caddy (`2010`) is the canonical ingress**. Remaining: confirm or repurpose `ubuntu-nginxrpm` (`105`).
- **Lab containers.** `prod-lockbits` (`310`), `preprod-lockbits` (`320`), and all three `debian-test-edragent` containers (`331`–`333`) deleted. `postgresql` secondary (`113`), `debian` (`1005`), GLPI (`1101`) deleted. Closed.
- **Wiki deployment.** This site now deploys via Dokploy on `obiwan` and is exposed at `homelab.cleeryy.cloud` through Caddy. See [Operations > Dokploy](/operations/dokploy).

## Documentation: Docmost → Fumadocs

- Docmost runs as LXC `109` on `pve1`.
- This Fumadocs site is the canonical documentation surface going forward.
- Action: migrate any surviving Docmost content into Fumadocs, then decide whether to keep Docmost online for archive access or decommission the LXC.

## Ingress: remaining `ubuntu-nginxrpm` (`105`)

- VM `105` still runs on `pve1`. Its role next to Caddy is not confirmed.
- Action: confirm whether anything still routes through it; decommission or repurpose.

## Network: finish the VLAN migration

- Production (20) and Management (50) carry the platform; Admin (10) is lightly used; Preproduction (25), IoT (30), Guest (40), Home (60) are configured but near idle.
- Legacy guests (all `pve1` Docker VMs, TrueNAS, NAS) still sit on `192.168.1.0/24`.
- Action: move remaining guests onto the VLAN plan, then write the inter-VLAN firewall policy (current `[DEV]`/`TEMP` allow-all rules are not final).

## Security: SSO coverage

- Authentik `forward_auth` protects selected Caddy routes (e.g. `code`). Most public routes, including this wiki, are currently open.
- Action: decide the default posture (SSO-by-default vs. explicit public list) and roll `forward_auth` out per route.

## Monitoring: Beszel → Prometheus/Grafana

- Prometheus (`566`) + Grafana (`567`) + PVE exporter (`576`) are live on the private zone; Beszel (`202`) still runs.
- Action: confirm Grafana as canonical, define alerting/notification channels, then decommission Beszel.
