Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

This is a flat view of where services live today. It is descriptive — not aspirational. Group is the role family, host is the Proxmox node, kind is VM or LXC.

Read it as a map, not a verdict. The two clear patterns are worth naming: everything new lands on `pve3` as an LXC on a VLAN, while the Docker VMs (now on production VLAN `10.1.20.0/24`) stay mostly parked on `pve1`. Nothing here implies a service *should* move — moves are tracked as migrations, and until one is decided the observed placement is simply the truth to operate against.

## Edge and ingress

Edge and ingress are concentrated on `pve3` because they must sit next to the firewall and the VLAN trunks. The table below lists the five hosts that move packets in and out, and its brevity reflects a deliberate consolidation around Caddy.

| Service | Kind | ID | Host | Notes |
|---------|------|----|------|-------|
| OPNsense | VM | `501` | `pve3` | Edge router, FW 26.1.x — WAN `192.168.1.250`, LAN trunk + 7 VLANs |
| Caddy | LXC | `2010` | `pve3` | Canonical ingress, `10.1.20.10` — public + private wildcard zones |
| Nginx (legacy) | VM | `105` | `pve1` | `ubuntu-nginxrpm`, role to be confirmed |
| cloudflared | LXC | `103` | `pve1` | Outbound tunnel |
| cloudflared-opnsense | LXC | `511` | `pve3` | Tunnel for edge-adjacent services |

Nginx Proxy Manager (VM `100`) has been decommissioned, so Caddy stands alone as canonical ingress and the legacy `105` entry above is the only remaining question mark in this group.

## Identity and access

Identity and access services are co-located on `pve3` so that SSO, bastion and DNS share fate with the ingress they protect. The table below is small because each of these services is meant to be singular.

| Service | Kind | ID | Host | Notes |
|---------|------|----|------|-------|
| Authentik (SSO) | LXC | `130` | `pve3` | SSO provider, `authentik.cleeryy.cloud` |
| Warpgate (bastion) | LXC | `222` | `pve3` | SSO SSH bastion, `10.1.20.22` |
| Guacamole | LXC | `106` | `pve3` | Remote access gateway |
| Technitium (DNS) | LXC | `553` | `pve3` | Internal DNS, `10.1.50.53` |

The operational point of gathering all four on the production and management VLANs of `pve3` is that a single-node outage takes down login, shell and name resolution together, which is exactly the recovery order the restore plan must assume.

## Notes

Two conventions apply across every table on this page and are restated here so they are not missed. "Beszel label" and "IP" come from Beszel's host list and apply only to the Docker host VMs, while LXC IPs follow the tag convention (`20.x` to `10.1.20.x`, `50.x` to `10.1.50.x`, `1.x` to `192.168.1.x`) and are confirmed only where a service binding, NAT rule, or DNS record pins them. This table is observed placement, not target placement: there is no implied recommendation to move any service in this view, and in-flight transitions belong to the [migration plan](/architecture/migration-plan).
