| Management LAN | `192.168.1.0/24` | `vmbr0` on both nodes |
| Internal / routed | `10.0.0.0/24` (at least) | `vmbr1` on `pve3`, via OPNsense `net1` |

OPNsense VM `401` connects `net0` to `vmbr0` and `net1` to `vmbr1`.
