## Notes

- LXCs are the dominant pattern for platform services. Multi-container application stacks are still deployed inside the Ubuntu VMs documented under [Virtual machines](/inventory/virtual-machines), not directly on Proxmox.
- LXC IPs follow the tag convention (`1.x` → `192.168.1.x`, `20.x` → `10.1.20.x`, `25.x` → `10.1.25.x`, `50.x` → `10.1.50.x`); see [Networks](/inventory/networks).
- `108` `alpine-docker` is documented as a generic Docker host; its specific workloads, if any, are not captured.
