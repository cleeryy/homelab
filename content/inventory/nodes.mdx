Last synced with live infrastructure: **September 2026**.

Two machines, two personalities. `pve1` is the old workhorse — modest, lightly loaded, carrying the legacy VMs nobody has had a reason to move. `pve3` is where everything happens now: twice the threads, nearly triple the RAM, and the load average to prove it, hosting the firewall, the platform services, and the media stack all at once. That imbalance is intentional but worth watching; if `pve3` ever looks full, the answer is not "add a third node" but "finish moving the legacy VMs off `pve1`'s shadow".

## Active nodes

| Node | Role | Proxmox VE | Management | Gateway |
|------|------|-----------|------------|---------|
| `pve1` | Infrastructure and management | 9.2.11 (kernel `7.0.14-8-pve`) | `192.168.1.101/24` on `vmbr0` | `192.168.1.1` |
| `pve3` | Primary compute and production | 9.2.11 (kernel `7.0.14-15-pve`) | `192.168.1.103/24` on `vmbr0` | `192.168.1.1` |

### `pve1`

- Hardware: 2x Intel Xeon E5-2450 v2 @ 2.50 GHz (32 threads), 64 GB RAM. Legacy BIOS boot.
- Bridges: `vmbr0` (management LAN).
- Storage attached: `local`, `local-lvm`, `pve1-storage` (ZFS), `nas` + `truenas` (shared NFS), `pbs`.
- Hosts legacy application VMs running Docker stacks, the code/database stack, and utility LXCs.
- Lightly loaded at last observation (loadavg ~2.7, ~37 GB RAM used). Uptime ~15 days.

### `pve3`

- Hardware: 2x Intel Xeon Silver 4208 @ 2.10 GHz (32 threads), ~172 GiB RAM. EFI boot.
- Bridges: `vmbr0` only (`vmbr1`/`vmbr2` are gone). Static route `10.1.0.0/16 via 10.1.50.1` persists for reaching the VLANs.
- Storage attached: `local`, `local-lvm` (large LVM-thin pool), `nas` + `truenas` (shared NFS), `pbs`.
- Additional physical disks passed through to the TrueNAS VM (`127`).
- No active ZFS pool on the host.
- Heavily loaded at last observation (loadavg 34–51, ~103 GB RAM used, ~1.1 GB swap used). Uptime ~3.8 days.
