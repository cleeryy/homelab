## ZFS pools

| Pool | Host | Role |
|------|------|------|
| `pve1-storage` | `pve1` | Application data on the infra node |

`pve3` runs no native ZFS pool. The TrueNAS VM owns the ZFS layer on the production node via passed-through disks.

## TrueNAS

- VM `127` `truenas` on `pve3` (`192.168.1.74`), ~8.5 GB RAM in use.
- Multiple physical disks passed through.
- Exposes `/mnt/raw/pve` over NFS (mounted as the `truenas` storage on both nodes) and serves SMB shares to the LAN.
- The exact share/dataset list is not captured here.

## Known gaps

Usable capacity per pool, per-guest disk allocation, and the physical disk inventory (model, serial, size, slot) — the three tables that turn "storage works" into "storage is planned". All three are evenings of careful `lsblk` away.
